Senior Distributed Systems Engineer - Stripe Infrastructure
Location: San Francisco, CA / Remote

Our Distributed Systems Infrastructure team designs, builds, and operates the distributed consensus engines, transaction routing backbones, and real-time ledger synchronization layers that handle over 12 billion requests a day.

What You Will Do:

- Drive database modernization initiatives moving from monolithic RDBMS to distributed SQL (CockroachDB).

Required Qualifications:
- 5+ years of experience engineering high-scale distributed systems and consensus protocols (Raft, Paxos).
- Proficiency with systems languages such as Rust, Go, or C++.
- Deep experience with distributed databases (e.g. CockroachDB, Spanner, Cassandra) and message queues (Kafka).
- Proven track record of improving P99 latency and reliability for high-volume event streams.

Preferred Qualifications:
- Experience with Kubernetes container orchestration at scale.
- Contributions to open-source distributed systems or storage engines.
- Bachelor's or Master's degree in Computer Science or equivalent practical experience.
